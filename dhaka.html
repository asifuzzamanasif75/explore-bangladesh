Dhaka

The vibrant capital city of Bangladesh, where history and modern urban life meet.

Life in Dhaka

Dhaka is one of the country's most important centers for government, education, business, technology, and culture. The city has a fast-moving atmosphere, with traditional markets and historic areas existing alongside modern offices, shopping centers, and universities.

Visitors can explore landmarks such as Lalbagh Fort, Ahsan Manzil, and the National Parliament area. Dhaka is also known for its diverse food culture, including biryani, traditional snacks, sweets, and many regional cuisines.

Historic Dhaka

Old Dhaka contains historic buildings, narrow streets, markets, mosques, and long-established food traditions.

Modern Dhaka

Newer parts of the city feature offices, shopping malls, restaurants, universities, and growing technology communities.
