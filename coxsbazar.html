Cox's Bazar

Home to one of the world's best-known long natural sandy sea beaches.

The Coastal Experience

Cox's Bazar is a popular coastal destination in southeastern Bangladesh. The area is known for its long beach, sea views, sunsets, seafood, and tourism facilities. Visitors often spend time walking along the shoreline and exploring nearby attractions.

The wider region also provides access to places such as Himchari and Inani Beach. A trip to Cox's Bazar can combine relaxation by the sea with short excursions to nearby hills, viewpoints, and local markets.

Sea Beach

The wide shoreline provides space for walking, photography, and enjoying the sea breeze.

Coastal Views

Sunrise and sunset create memorable views along the coast and nearby viewpoints.
