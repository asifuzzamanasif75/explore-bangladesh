Bangladesh Heritage

Historic monuments and cultural traditions that reflect the country's long and diverse history.

History & Culture

Bangladesh has a rich cultural and architectural heritage shaped by many historical periods. Archaeological sites, mosques, temples, forts, palaces, and traditional crafts provide evidence of the country's changing societies and artistic traditions.

Places such as the historic mosque city of Bagerhat, the ruins of Paharpur, and the old Mughal-era architecture of Dhaka are important parts of the national heritage. Cultural festivals, music, literature, and traditional foods continue to connect the past with contemporary life.
