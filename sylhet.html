Sylhet

A green northeastern region famous for tea gardens, rivers, wetlands, and scenic landscapes.

Green Sylhet

Sylhet is widely associated with lush tea gardens, rolling landscapes, streams, and haor wetlands. The region has a distinctive natural environment that attracts visitors looking for quieter scenery away from major urban centers.

Popular places around Sylhet include Ratargul Swamp Forest, Jaflong, and tea-growing areas. Local traditions, regional cuisine, and the landscape together make Sylhet an interesting destination for nature-focused travel.

Tea Gardens

Tea estates cover many green hills and provide one of the region's most recognizable landscapes.

Forest & Water

Forests, streams, rivers, and wetlands provide a variety of natural environments for visitors to explore.
